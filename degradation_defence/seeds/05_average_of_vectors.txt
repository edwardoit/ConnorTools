Take three vectors v1, v2, v3 in space. Form two weighted averages w1 = a1 v1 + a2 v2 + a3 v3 and w2 = b1 v1 + b2 v2 + b3 v3, where the weights in each average are nonnegative and add up to 1. Show that if both averages use equal weights 1/3, then w1 = w2, and explain why the two vectors then span no area.
